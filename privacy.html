Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Olivia OLIYAR is a private assistant used only by its owner. It accesses Google Calendar data of the owner's own Google account, only after the owner grants consent, and only to list availability and to create or update the owner's calendar events and Google Meet links.

  • Data is used only to manage the owner's schedule. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner's account.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
